# Plumbing Enquiry Engine — Decisions

## Product
- Product: Zayzana Plumbing Enquiry Engine
- Purpose: Turn incoming plumbing enquiries into organised, qualified opportunities.
- Core workflow: Capture -> Qualify -> Prioritise -> Route -> Follow Up.
- The prototype is intentionally small, clean and sellable.

## Commercial
- Target market: Plumbing and other service businesses.
- Initial overseas focus: UK, USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
- Positioning: Improve the workflow around existing systems rather than replacing them.
- Primary commercial objective: secure a pilot/customer and validate the workflow.

## Operating Rules
- Do not pitch a generic AI chatbot.
- Do not replace systems unnecessarily.
- Do not build features without a real commercial reason.
- Use prospect feedback to guide future development.
- Prototype V1 is frozen until a real prospect/customer gives us a reason to change it.

## Zayzana Test
Every meaningful next step should:
1. Make money.
2. Create an owned reusable asset.
3. Make the next opportunity easier.
