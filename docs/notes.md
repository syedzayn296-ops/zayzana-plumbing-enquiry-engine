## Prototype
- Single-page browser prototype.
- Demonstrates enquiry capture, qualification, prioritisation, routing and follow-up.
- Designed for laptop-based prospect demonstrations.
- No production integrations yet.

## Commercial Experiment
- Overseas plumbing campaign uses five markets:
  - UK
  - USA
  - Canada
  - Australia
  - New Zealand
- Campaign target: 100 prospects.
- Initial outreach completed to 80 prospects.
- 20 prospects held because a verified contact/email was not available or the prospect was deliberately held.
- Follow-up rule:
  - Day 0: initial email
  - Day 5 business day: first follow-up
  - Day 10-12 business days: final follow-up
  - Then stop and move to nurture/watch.
- Follow-ups should be reviewed before sending.
- No mass automatic chasing.
